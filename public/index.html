What This App Does

Your calendar, working while you're on the job

When a customer calls or texts a business that uses LvL Up Media's 24/7 Digital Booking Hub, the AI phone assistant needs to know what times are actually open. lvlupbookings is the piece that makes that possible — it reads free/busy availability from the connected Google Calendar and writes new appointments directly onto it once a time is confirmed with the customer.

Nothing is booked without a real, available time slot, and every appointment created through the assistant is placed on the same calendar the business already uses — no separate scheduling tool to check.

Your Data

How we handle calendar access

lvlupbookings only requests the calendar permissions it needs to check availability and create appointments. It does not read, modify, or delete any other calendar data, and access can be revoked at any time from a connected Google account's third-party access settings.
